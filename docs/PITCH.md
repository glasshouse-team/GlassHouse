Three minutes on stage: a spoken pitch over a live demo, then judge questions. This file is the source for what we say and click; docs/DEMO.md holds the stage setup. Facts come from docs/DECISIONS.md (D-009, D-010, D-017, D-026, D-027, D-028) and the measured results in PRs #13, #20, #22 and #25.

**Rule for the whole pitch: claim only what shipped.** Before the final rehearsal, run the readiness gate below and switch every segment whose feature is not on `main` to its cut version. Never describe a feature on stage that the audience can't see working, and never say "walk anywhere": the tour is one 3D world per room spot, linked by our doorway arrows (D-009).

## The pitch (spoken, 3:00)

What each person says, in order. The click-by-click script below has the same times.

**0:00-0:20 Temuri (hook).** "Ever arrived somewhere that didn't look like the photos? You can't trust a listing or picture its layout. Glasshouse rebuilds home rental as if AI was there from day one: three agents work for you, and every home is a 3D model that shows which parts are real."

**0:20-0:45 Beka (guest agent and Verifier).** "I just describe the trip. My guest agent reads every listing and ranks them, and each reason cites a fact. Those facts come from the third agent, the Verifier, which works for nobody. It read every listing's photos and compared them with the text. This flat says 'sea view'. No photo shows the sea, so the Verifier flags it, with the photos as evidence."

**0:45-1:25 Temuri (the honest 3D).** "This is my own apartment, built from 8 photos per room. We stand in each room and the arrows take us through the doorways: entrance, kitchen, living room. Where would I work? The desk is right here. Now the honest part: one kitchen wall was never photographed, and the AI filled it in. It invented a door, an air conditioner and cables that aren't there. Glasshouse doesn't hide that: it marks it."

**1:25-2:05 Beka (negotiation, Temuri holds the host phone).** "Back to our Kazbegi house: $520 for four nights. Can I pay $50 less? I tell my agent my target and, privately, the most I'd pay. The host's agent has its own private floor. They negotiate live: $470, $495, $480. Neither agent sees the other's limits, and no LLM touches the totals: code computes every price and checks every offer. $480 is below the host's auto-accept level, so a person decides: Giorgi's phone asks, he taps Approve. Booked, $528 with the fee."

**2:05-2:40 Temuri (host side).** "Hosts get the other half. The Verifier found the blank kitchen wall and told the host exactly what to shoot: two photos of that wall. The new version has no invented door, and its coverage score goes up. Every step you've seen, agent or human, is in this live feed."

**2:40-3:00 Beka (close).** "Three agents, one rule: show what's real. The problem: you can't trust or picture a home before you book. Our answer: a 3D home that admits what it doesn't know, a Verifier that catches lies, and agents that bargain inside rules written in code. Two of us built it in 24 hours. Thank you."

## Backup plan

- **One failed retry, then video.** Each segment has one retry. After that, say "here is the same flow recorded an hour ago" and play the backup video from that segment's start time (chapters at 0:20, 0:45, 1:25, 2:05).
- **Venue Wi-Fi down:** run `pnpm dev` locally with cached splats; the brief and the negotiation need the Claude API, so for those go to the backup video.
- **Phone fails:** every phone step also works on the laptop's host tab.
- **Claude API slow or rate-limited:** the X-ray, the gap note and the 3D read saved rows and never call a model on a page view; only the brief and the negotiation are live. Video for those two.
- **A judge asks to see something live after the demo:** the X-ray panel and the feed are the safest; both read saved data.

## Questions judges will ask

One sentence each; the fuller line after the dash is for a follow-up.

| Question                                              | Answer                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Isn't the 3D just hallucinated?                       | Partly, and we show exactly where — our kitchen v1 was missing two photos and the model invented a door, an air conditioner and cables; the Verifier finds those blank spots, the overlay marks them and the host is told what to shoot.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Why should I trust the Verifier?                      | It works for neither side and has to show its evidence — every fact and contradiction cites the listing's own photos, code drops anything without evidence or with an unknown photo id, it caught both seeded lies in 3 of 3 runs; on our own apartment, whose photos we know, it raised 0 contradictions in 3 of 3 runs; on the stock-photo listings the 24 flags left are missing evidence, such as a view or bed count no photo shows; and it never books, prices or decides anything. |
| How much does an X-ray cost?                          | One pass over all 11 listings is about 87k input and 23k output tokens: at list prices about 2 cents if every listing ran on Haiku 5.5 and about 40 cents if every listing ran on Sonnet 5.5 (we use Sonnet only on the two hero homes) — it runs once per listing change and is saved, never on a page view.                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Can the agent give the house away?                    | No — floors, the guest's maximum and the round limit are checked in code (`validateOffer`) before an offer is saved, prices come from `computeQuote`, and anything below the host's auto-accept level needs a person's tap.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Can one side see the other's limits?                  | No — each agent's context is built by separate code from separate secret tables, and tests plant odd numbers to prove they never reach the other side's prompt.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| How long and how much per 3D home?                    | About 6-9 minutes and 1,600 World Labs credits per room spot, rooms in parallel, so about 8,000 credits for a 5-room home — generated once per version and served from our storage.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| How accurate is the floor plan?                       | Only if GH-18 shipped: approximate, scaled from the 3D model's metric estimate. Say nothing about calibration unless GH-18 v2 shipped.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Why not the big platform's own AI?                    | Their AI ranks and summarizes; ours acts for each side, negotiates within rules enforced in code, and checks the listing against its own photos.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| What if a listing's text tries to instruct the agent? | Guest, host and listing text goes to the models as data, never as instructions, and every model output is validated with a schema before we use it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Privacy?                                              | Owners consent, personal items are removed before photographing, photos are stripped of GPS data, and host limits and guest budgets live in tables the browser can't read.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| What did you build vs use?                            | We used World Labs Marble for 3D reconstruction, Claude for the agents and vision, Supabase and Next.js; we built the three agents, the Verifier's X-ray, the per-room tour with doorway arrows, the negotiation engine and validators, and the live feed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

Drop answers about calibration (GH-18 v2), guided capture (GH-22) and check-in verification (GH-23) unless they shipped.
