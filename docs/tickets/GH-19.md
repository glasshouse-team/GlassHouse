## Build notes

- The rig writes `screens/manifest.json` (`fovDeg`, `size`, and viewpoints with id, position, room and per-view quaternions). Node rebuilds each camera and raycasts the box centers; unit-test that on a box mesh.
- Render 512 x 512 with `dpr={1}` and `preserveDrawingBuffer`; after each move, wait until Spark has finished sorting (a stale sort looks smeared). Upload through a new `POST /api/worlds/[id]/screens` that returns signed upload URLs.
- Route: at most 5 viewpoints per call (300 s limit). `pnpm gaps <worldId>` runs them all (concurrency 4, at most 40 viewpoints). Real frames: `listing_photos` of the viewpoint's room.
- Rays miss in blank areas: place the gap 2 m along the ray. Re-runs delete open gaps and auto object anchors first. Object anchors: `position` = eye, `look_at` = hit.
- Record now: `POST /api/captures { kind: "fill_in", gapId }`, a signed upload to `captures`, then gap status `requested`. In DEMO_MODE it stops there.

## Goal
After a world is generated, the Verifier finds blank, smeared or invented areas. The host agent turns each one into a note plus a pin in 3D: "Kitchen: the corner left of the fridge is blank. Stand at the pin and record 10 seconds, turning slowly." This is the answer to "isn't the 3D just hallucinated?"

**When:** hours 15–18 · **Estimate:** 3h · **Depends on:** GH-10, GH-11, GH-18 (rooms) · **Blocks:** GH-21 (overlay reads gaps)

## Build
1. **Screenshot rig page** `/tools/rig?world=<id>` (`app/tools/rig/`, hidden):
    - **Viewpoints:** a grid every 1.5 m over the floor, inside the room polygons or wall bounds. Keep only points where a downward ray hits the floor and an upward ray hits the ceiling within 3.5 m.
    - **Views:** camera at 1.5 m height, 6 directions 60° apart, 90° field of view, each rendered to a 512 × 512 offscreen target.
    - Upload views to `media/worlds/{id}/screens/{view}.jpg`.
    - It runs in a laptop browser for the hero homes, and in the host's browser after a new generation.
2. **Analyze** (`tools/verifier/gaps.ts`, `POST /api/worlds/[id]/gaps`):
    - Per viewpoint, send the 6 views + the 4 nearest source frames (by room) to `MODEL_AGENT`.
    - Zod output per view: `issue` (none | blank | smeared | stretched | invented | dark), `description`, `bbox` (normalized), `severity` 1–3.
    - Also list visible objects (desk, bed, sofa, window, balcony door, TV, kitchen) with boxes. These become object anchors for GH-20.
3. **Place in 3D:**
    - Cast a ray from the viewpoint through the box center into the collider mesh BVH; the hit is the gap position.
    - Merge gaps within 1 m and assign each a room (from GH-18).
    - Write the `gaps` rows and the object `anchors`.
4. **Coverage score:** views with no issue ÷ all views → `worlds.coverage_score`.
5. **Host notes** (`components/gaps/GapTasks.tsx`, host dashboard slot):
    - One card per gap: room, plain instruction, screenshot, a "Show in 3D" button, and a "Record now" button (file input with `capture="environment"` until GH-22 exists).
    - Each note logs an event: "Host agent asked Giorgi for 2 more clips".
6. **Pins in the tour:** amber camera pins at open gaps, via `useTour().addOverlay` from inside Temuri's own tour components.
7. **Regenerate (v1 shortcut):** for the demo, the fill-in version is the pre-generated Hero 1 v2 from GH-6. If time allows, wire "Record now" → GH-10 creates a new version from the walkthrough + fill-in (join clips, or frames to HY World).
8. **Compare and publish:** the host toggles v1 and v2, sees the coverage score rise, and publishes.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Hero 1 v1: the deliberately skipped kitchen corner is found and pinned in the right place (within about 1 m).
- [ ] Hero 1 v2 has a higher coverage score than v1, and that gap is gone.
- [ ] No more than 3 false gaps in clean rooms on Hero 2 (check by eye; tune the prompt).
- [ ] Object anchors created for the desk, bed and sofa in both hero homes.
