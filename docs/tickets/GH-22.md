## Goal
Hosts record a good walkthrough without help: instructions, a room checklist, a compass ring that fills as they turn, and live warnings. Until this exists, hosts upload a plain video with a typed room list.

**Start only after GH-17 says all should-haves are done.** · **Estimate:** 3–4h · **Depends on:** GH-10

## Build (`app/host/capture/[listingId]`, `components/capture/`)
1. **Instructions screen:**
    - Lights on, doors open, remove personal items, walk slowly, phone at chest height, full turn in the middle of each room.
    - An **"Allow camera and motion"** button. iOS only grants compass access via `DeviceOrientationEvent.requestPermission()` called from a tap.
2. **Room checklist:** pre-filled from bedrooms and baths (Entrance, Living room, Kitchen, Bedroom 1, Bathroom…), editable.
3. **Recorder:**
    - `getUserMedia({video: {facingMode: 'environment', width: 1280, height: 720, frameRate: 30}, audio: true})`.
    - `MediaRecorder` with a type picked by `isTypeSupported`: `video/mp4` (Safari) or `video/webm` (Chrome).
4. **Overlay while recording:**
    - Current room and a "Next room" button that saves `{room, t}` into `room_markers`.
    - A ring filling with the degrees covered, from the compass heading saved into `heading_track`.
    - Warnings: turning too fast, too dark (average frame brightness sampled every 500 ms via canvas), shaky.
    - A target of 60–90 s in total.
5. **Upload:** Supabase resumable upload with a progress bar, resuming after a drop.
6. **Fill-in mode:** the same recorder for one spot, showing that gap's screenshot faintly over the camera to aim.
7. **Then:** a quality report → "Generate my 3D home" (GH-10) → progress → gap notes (GH-19).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Works over HTTPS on iPhone Safari and Android Chrome (test with ngrok or Vercel).
- [ ] A full capture uploads and starts a generation; room markers are saved.
- [ ] The compass ring works on both phones, or degrades gracefully if permission is denied.
