## Goal
Reviews become pins in the 3D: "This window faces a noisy street" sits on that window.

**Only if ahead of schedule.** · **Estimate:** 2h · **Depends on:** GH-11, GH-19 (object anchors), GH-7 (placeable review complaints)

## Build
1. `scripts/split-reviews.ts`: Haiku 5.5 splits each review into claims (text, sentiment, room or object), then matches each claim to the nearest anchor → `review_claims`.
2. In the tour: pins rendered with drei `<Html>` (green for praise, amber for complaints). Clicking one shows the review excerpt and its author.
3. The X-ray panel shows review/listing conflicts ("3 reviews mention street noise; the listing says quiet").

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] At least 5 claims pinned correctly on Hero home 1; clicking one shows the source review.
