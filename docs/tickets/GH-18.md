## Goal
Guests see a real floor plan next to the gallery and as a minimap inside the tour. The geometry comes from the collider mesh in **meters** (Marble's `metric_scale_factor`); the Verifier only names the rooms.

**When:** hours 13–16 · **Estimate:** v1 2–3h, v2 +3h · **Depends on:** GH-10, GH-11 · **Blocks:** GH-19 (room polygons help the gap check), GH-21 (hatching)

## v1 (build this)
1. **Load** (`scripts/build-floorplans.ts` and `POST /api/worlds/[id]/floorplan`):
    - Read the stored collider mesh in Node (a GLB loads with three's `GLTFLoader`; check the format found in GH-3).
    - Multiply by `metric_scale_factor`, shift by `ground_plane_offset` → meters, floor at y = 0.
2. **Slice** (`lib/geometry/slice.ts`):
    - Cut the mesh with horizontal planes at 1.0, 1.3 and 1.6 m. Each triangle crossing a plane gives a 2D segment.
    - Use three-mesh-bvh shapecast so only triangles near each plane are tested.
3. **Wall map:**
    - Rasterize the segments into a 5 cm grid.
    - Keep cells hit in at least 2 of the 3 slices; this drops furniture tops and noise.
4. **Straighten** (`lib/geometry/walls.ts`):
    - Find the main wall angle from a histogram of segment angles and rotate it to the axes.
    - Snap segments within about 5° of an axis, merge collinear segments, drop pieces under 20 cm.
    - Treat these thresholds as tuning starting points.
5. **Name rooms:**
    - Render a **top-down orthographic view** of the splat with a clipping plane at 1.8 m (ceiling removed) at 1024 px.
    - Send it to `MODEL_AGENT` (Sonnet 5.5) together with the room list from GH-6 and one frame per room.
    - It returns `[{room, x, y}]` label positions in image pixels; convert them to world meters.
    - Save room anchors at those points (`anchors.kind = 'room'`, source `auto`). These feed Jump to room.
6. **Draw the SVG** (`lib/geometry/svg.ts`):
    - Thick walls, room names, a scale bar, and a "Plan is approximate" note.
    - Hatching on areas near open gaps (read from `gaps`, empty until GH-19).
    - Save it to `media/worlds/{id}/floorplan.svg`, plus a `floor_plans` row (`version = 'v1'`, `scale_source = 'marble-metric'`).
7. **UI:**
    - `components/floorplan/FloorPlanCard.tsx` (listing slot) shows the plan; clicking a room opens the tour at that room.
    - `components/floorplan/Minimap.tsx` inside the tour shows a "you are here" dot and a view cone synced to the camera; clicking a room calls `flyTo`; `highlightRoom` lights a room.

## v2 (only if time is left after the freeze-critical work)
- Room polygons: close door gaps of about 1 m, flood-fill free space, keep areas over 2 m², build polygons with d3-contour + simplify-js.
- Areas in m² per room and in total; doors drawn as gaps with swing arcs.
- **Calibration:** the host taps a door and enters its real width, and the scale updates.

## Fallbacks
- (A) Slicing too noisy → ask Sonnet for room rectangles from the top-down render and draw a coarse plan.
- (B) Show the top-down render itself as "View from above".

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Hero homes 1 and 2: the plan shows straight walls, the room count matches reality, and each room is named correctly.
- [ ] The minimap dot follows the camera within about half a meter; clicking a room jumps there.
- [ ] Room anchors created automatically, and Jump to room uses them.
- [ ] Generated once and stored; the listing page loads the SVG from storage.
