## Goal

The wow moment: a guest walks through the whole house in the browser on laptop or phone. It also exposes the `useTour()` API that Beka's add-ons (Ask the space, honesty overlay) build on.

**Owner:** Temuri · **When:** hours 7–11 · **Estimate:** 4h · **Depends on:** GH-5, GH-10 · **Blocks:** GH-18, GH-19, GH-20, GH-21

## Build

### 1. Route `/tour/[listingId]` (`app/(guest)/tour/`)

Loads the published world (or `?version=n` for host preview).

### 2. Scene (`components/tour/Viewer.tsx`)

- `<Canvas gl={{ antialias: false }}>` (Spark's recommendation) with a `SparkRenderer` and a `SplatMesh` from `spz_url`.
- On phones, use the web-size splat. Add an "HQ" toggle on desktop.
- **Metric scale:** multiply by `metric_scale_factor`, then shift down by `ground_plane_offset`, so 1 unit = 1 m and the floor is at y = 0. Fix the orientation if the splat loads upside down.

### 3. Collisions

- Load the collider mesh invisibly and build a BVH (`three-mesh-bvh`).
- Raycast forward to stop 0.3 m before walls; raycast down to keep the eye height at 1.6 m above the floor.

### 4. Controls

- **Desktop:** pointer lock + WASD/arrows; Shift to run.
- **Phone:** an on-screen joystick and drag to look.
- A "Dollhouse" toggle orbits the whole house from above.

### 5. Jump to room

A menu of `anchors` with `kind = 'room'`. Until GH-18 creates them, use manual anchors.

### 6. Anchor mode (`?anchors=1`, dev only)

Click a surface → the raycast hit is saved as `{name, kind, position, look_at}` in `anchors`. Use it to label "desk", "bed", "balcony", "kitchen" and each room on both hero homes (about 10 minutes per home).

### 7. Room badges

"Built from real footage" or "Partly AI-filled", read from `gaps` (empty until GH-19).

### 8. Loading and errors

Thumbnail + progress bar while the splat streams; a friendly error with photo-gallery fallback if WebGL fails.

### 9. `components/tour/TourContext.tsx`: implement `useTour()` exactly as frozen in GH-5

- `flyTo` uses drei `CameraControls.setLookAt(..., true)` for a smooth transition.
- `addOverlay(obj)` adds to the scene and returns a remove function.
- `highlightRoom(name)` is used by the minimap and Ask the space.

### 10. `components/tour/TourLauncher.tsx`

Replaces the stub on the listing page: a thumbnail card with "Walk through this home".
